// 01 — Selected Projects
01

Vision-Driven Robotic Manipulator

PythonMediaPipeArduinoMG996RSolidWorks3D Print

A 4-DOF robotic arm that mirrors live hand motion in real time — from camera to servo, in one integrated pipeline.

MediaPipe extracts hand landmarks via Python, which serializes joint angles to an Arduino controlling MG996R servos. Structural components are custom-designed in SolidWorks and 3D printed. Key engineering challenges: insufficient holding torque and mechanical wobble under load, resolved by shortening arm length to reduce joint moment and redesigning link geometry for rigidity.

  • 4-DOF arm designed around MG996R servo actuation
  • Python + MediaPipe pipeline → serial → live servo control
  • Iterated 3D printed components to eliminate oscillation and instability
02

Computer Vision Tracking Turret

PythonArduinoServosTracking

A pan/tilt turret that locks onto a hand target in real time using camera-based vision.

Camera feed → Python processing → microcontroller actuation, with servo tuning to balance responsiveness against jitter. Simpler platform than the manipulator, but focused specifically on tracking performance and control latency.

  • Vision-guided pan/tilt platform for live target following
  • Servo speed and update rate tuned to minimize oscillation

04

Hand-Crank Generator Experimental

ElectromechanicalPrototyping

Manual energy generation prototype exploring mechanical-to-electrical conversion.

Hand-driven crank connected to a generator. Testing revealed the generator requires far greater RPM than direct hand cranking can provide — gearing is essential for any viable power output. A clear, documented failure with identified solution path.

  • Built and tested manual crank-driven generator
  • Identified mechanical advantage as critical missing component
